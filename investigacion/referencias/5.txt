7 grams protein
Seven grams of protein per serving, because good looks only get you so far. Turns out these spicy little numbers have some muscle behind them too.
2 grams of fiber
A little fiber never hurt anybody’s reputation. Each serving brings 2 grams along for the ride, because even scandalous snacks can have some substance.
Wing Flavored
